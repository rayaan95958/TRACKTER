1.INSIDE CODE->COMPUTER VISION->  (i)DATASET CREATION- Codes for selection frames from recorded video, cleaning and annotation.
                                  (ii)KEY FRAME SELECTION- A single frame is selected per part that is passed onto product identification
                                  (iii)PRODUCT IDENTIFICATION- Takes the key frame and identifies what part it is.

2.PRODUCT IDENTIFICATION- ResNet-18 trained model for identification of the part from the passed key frame and then deletes that key frame from the folder.

3.KEY FRAME SELECTION- (i)The base model for object detection from real-time video is YoloV4-tiny.
                       (ii)Then we use "Lucas-Kaneda tracking algorithm" for tracking.
                       (iii)KFS of the frame with nearly centred bounding box.

4.The MASTER_CONTROLLER orchestrates the entire codebase.
